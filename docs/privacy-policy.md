# Privacy Policy for dEdx

**Last updated: April 26, 2026**

## No Data Collection

dEdx does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information. The app performs all calculations locally on your device.

Specifically, dEdx:

- Does not collect personal information of any kind
- Does not require an internet connection
- Does not use analytics or crash reporting services
- Does not use advertising
- Does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other device sensors
- Does not store any data outside of the app itself
